# Privacy Policy

PlayCap runs 100% offline. Zero telemetry.

- No data collection, no analytics, no crash reporting
- No network access of any kind (the app makes no network requests)
- No accounts, no sign-up
- All settings and usage history stay on the Mac, in `/Library/Application Support/PlayCap/`

Purchases of the prebuilt package are handled by Gumroad; their privacy policy applies to the checkout only. The app itself never talks to Gumroad or anyone else.

You can verify all of this in the source code in this repository.
